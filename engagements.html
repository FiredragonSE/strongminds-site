The work

One country. One programme. A sales engine that moves.

Technology and defence companies call when a board wants an answer, a bid is coming, or a country has been discussed for a year and nothing has moved. The fee is fixed before the work starts.

01

Lead generation

We identify and open the right accounts. Not lists. Named buyers, partners and programme owners who have a reason to take the call.

  • Who purchases, who influences, and who already sits in the channel.
  • Competitors and where you sit among them.
  • Political and stakeholder maps when the buyer is a public body.
What you getA written brief. The people are named. Evidence and judgement are kept apart.
02

Meetings

The first conversations that matter. Prepared, relevant, and with someone who has already done the reading. We work from Stockholm across Europe and the Nordics.

  • The partners and buyers the earlier work has already given a reason to meet.
  • Partnership talks with a point to them.
  • Your own commercial lead still owning the account when we step back.
What you getMeetings that were worth having, and a note of what was said.
03

Contracts

The path from interest to agreement. What the buyer needs to see, and how the offer is described so a specifier, a ministry or a board can hold it and move it forward.

  • How the programme runs and the path the buyer walks.
  • Whether a partner or a subcontract is the sensible shape.
  • Language a specifier or a board can actually use.
What you getA path toward the agreement, including what to do before the formal process opens.
04

Closing

The final steps that turn a discussion into a signed deal. Clear, direct, and without unnecessary delay. Waiting is a legitimate answer if the market is not ready.

  • The risks that would stop you, set apart from the ones you can live with.
  • A sequence for the first ninety days once the route is clear.
What you getSomething you can brief from. If the honest answer is not this market yet, you will have that in writing.

What we turn down

  • Fundraising, placing a fund, or writing an investor story with no operating question under it.
  • Coaching a founder, or writing a growth plan for a company that has not chosen a country or a programme.
  • Becoming your business-development department. This is a focused practice.
  • A question we cannot actually read. If it needs a specialist we are not, we say so before there is a fee.
