Small, on purpose.

Founder and CEO

Gergana Almquist, Ph.D.

Stockholm. We work in several languages. You deal with us, not a team behind a brand.

Strong Minds is our practice. Technology and defence companies come when they need clear market intelligence, a way into a European country, or a path onto a programme. If a question needs someone on the ground whom we trust, we bring them in. There is no junior layer between the brief and the judgement.

Gergana Almquist has a Ph.D. in Innovation Management from Copenhagen Business School, and a degree in International Business from the University of Cologne. Before this she worked in investment analysis and in business development, including on infrastructure and energy questions in Sweden. The useful part is that the same person reads the market and then stays with the entry.

Insight, energy, integrity is not a rotating slogan. If we cannot see the market, we should not take the work. If the brief never leaves the page, it was not finished. On a fixed fee, the scope is the scope: a weak market gets reported as weak.

What the small size means in practice

  • You hire us, not a brand with a delivery team behind it.
  • The fee is fixed and written down.
  • Stockholm as the base. The work covers Europe and the Nordics.
  • English is the language of the practice. Other languages are for the room you are trying to enter.
